The current version of this website does not use analytics or marketing cookies. We use only essential local storage to remember that you have read our privacy notice.

1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files that a website or associated service may store in your browser. Similar technologies, such as local storage (localStorage), can store simple preferences in your browser without using a traditional cookie.

Essential technologies may be used when needed to operate the website or a service explicitly requested by a user. Optional analytics or marketing technologies, when present and requiring consent, should not be activated until the user has made the appropriate choice.

2. What This Website Currently Uses

CategoryStatusPurpose
Essential Local StorageActiveRemembers that the cookie notice has been read and which version was shown.
Analytics CookiesNot UsedThe website currently does not use Google Analytics or another tool to track visitor behaviour.
Marketing CookiesNot UsedThe website currently does not use Meta Pixel or another marketing tracker.

If optional analytics or marketing tools are introduced in the future, this Policy and the consent mechanism must be updated before they are activated.

3. Essential Local Storage

The website stores a technical record in your browser named emili_privacy_notice_v1. The record contains the notice version, acknowledgement that it has been read and the time of your choice. It does not contain your name, email, telephone number or booking details and is not used to track you across different pages or websites.

If you clear website data in your browser settings, the notice may appear again.

4. External Services and Resources

The website uses external technical services for hosting and contact form delivery. It may also load web fonts from Google's infrastructure. Such network requests are not the same as setting a marketing cookie, although the provider may receive technical data necessary to deliver the resource, such as an IP address and browser information.

WhatsApp and Google Maps links are activated only when you click them. Once you leave the website, the external service's privacy and cookie policies apply.

5. Managing Settings

The footer contains a link to Cookie Settings which displays the current status of each category. As optional cookies are not currently used, there are no optional categories to accept or reject.

You can also clear or block cookies and local storage through your browser settings. Blocking essential storage may cause the notice to appear repeatedly or affect certain functions.

6. Changes to This Policy

The technologies listed here must reflect those actually used by the website. If these tools change, this Policy and any consent mechanism will be updated accordingly.
